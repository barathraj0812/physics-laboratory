What happens in a collision?

During contact, objects exchange impulse and redistribute momentum. In an isolated two-body system, total momentum is conserved. Kinetic energy is also conserved in an ideal elastic collision; in inelastic collisions, some becomes deformation, heat, or sound.

Two principles

Momentum: total before = total after for every collision type in this isolated model.
Kinetic energy: conserved only when the collision is elastic (e = 1); it decreases when e < 1.
