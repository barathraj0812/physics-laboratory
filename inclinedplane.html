Equations & Theory

Open the physics reference
Weight decomposition

W = mg acts vertically down. Parallel to ramp: mg sin θ downslope. Perpendicular: mg cos θ into the ramp. These are components of the same weight, not additional forces.

Normal force

N = mg cos θ, perpendicular to the surface and outward, assuming contact and no other normal forces.

Static friction and critical angle

At rest, fs adapts to balance mg sin θ up to fs,max = μsN. Rest is possible when tan θ ≤ μs. θc = tan⁻¹(μs) for 0°–80°.

Kinetic friction and acceleration

During sliding, fk = μkN opposes motion. Downslope: a = g(sin θ − μk cos θ). Upslope motion: a = g(sin θ + μk cos θ) in the positive downslope coordinate.

Energy and work

Set zero potential at the lower endpoint: h(s) = (L − s)sin θ. K = ½mv², U = mgh. Friction work Wf = ∫Ff ds ≤ 0; mechanical energy falls by this amount.

Sign convention and assumptions

s increases downslope; v > 0 is downslope and v < 0 upslope. A rigid block stays in contact with a straight, rigid incline. No air drag, rotation, rolling resistance, deformation, or thermal model.

Translational point-mass model. The 0.02 s motion step uses exact constant-acceleration updates, with analytic stopping and ramp-end event handling. Velocity tolerance is 10⁻⁸ m/s; the static threshold uses a 10⁻¹⁰ relative force tolerance.
