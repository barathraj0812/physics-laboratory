How a laboratory works

The platform is designed around this workflow. Features will vary between laboratories as they are built.

  1. Adjust physical parameters.
  2. Observe the simulation.
  3. Inspect vectors and measurements.
  4. Analyze graphs and data.
  5. Connect equations to observed behavior.
  6. Verify scientific relationships.
