Three-Body Orbit

Explore how three masses shape one another’s motion through gravity.

Interactive simulation
Three interacting bodies rarely follow simple closed-form paths. Each body accelerates under the combined gravitational influence of the other two, so trajectories are integrated numerically. Some configurations are periodic or hierarchical; arbitrary configurations are not guaranteed to be stable. Coordinates are in metres (x right, y up); the canvas uses screen pixels only for display.

Physics notes

Newtonian gravity follows an inverse-square law. Pairwise forces act in equal and opposite directions, and the acceleration of each body is the sum of the pulls from the other two. Momentum and angular momentum should remain nearly constant; mechanical energy drift depends on timestep and trajectory.
